TTQA (Turn the Question Around) Practice

TEKS 8.10(B) & 8.11(D) | Chop & Flip Prompt Framing

Let's Be Clear!

Never start an answer with "Because", "It shows that", or "Yes". Chop the question words off the front, flip the remaining phrase into an authoritative statement, and state your claim clearly!

👑 The Boss

Identify the question word (Why, How, What) and CHOP IT OFF.

🧾 The Proof

FLIP the remaining subject and verb into your claim sentence stem.

🔄 The Remix

Complete the statement with your direct answer—no fluff or dead weight.
